My Story

Made with heart,
seasoned with soul.

Heart N Soul was born from the kind of moments that stay with you — family gathered around the table, Southern dishes made with care, and the warmth that only a home‑cooked meal can bring. Those early memories shaped my love for cooking and taught me that food is one of the most powerful ways to bring people together.

This brand is built on that foundation. Heart N Soul is a celebration of flavor, culture, and connection. Every dish is crafted with intention, rooted in tradition, and elevated with the soul that makes it unforgettable.

My mission is simple: spread love, create joy, and make people feel seen, appreciated, and cared for — one meal at a time. I believe change begins with us, and for me, that change starts in the kitchen.

Heart N Soul means many things, but above all, it means love — the kind you can taste, feel, and remember.

As you explore our story, our food, and our journey, I hope Heart N Soul becomes part of yours. A moment of comfort. A spark of joy. A memory worth keeping.

Welcome to Heart N Soul — where every bite is made with heart and seasoned with soul.

Founder & Chef

Chef Dillon Jerrell

Chef Dillon blends fine dining technique with the bold, fire‑kissed flavors of backyard cooking. His approach is rooted in intention: every dish crafted with precision, passion, and the soulful touch that defines Heart N Soul.

From flame‑seared burgers to curated private dining experiences, Chef Dillon brings together artistry, authenticity, and heart in every plate.
